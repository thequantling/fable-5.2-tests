How to grill

  • Look around — drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan.
  • Cook — drag burgers and sausages from the paper onto the grate. The left side of the coals runs hotter.
  • Flip — tap a burger to flip it, tap a sausage to roll it. Grill marks sear in where the bars touch.
  • Serve — drag anything onto the enamel tray when it looks right. Leave it too long and it burns.
  • Stoke — tap the coals for a shower of sparks.
Everything is rendered live in your browser with three.js. Night-sky panorama, deck timber, table, buns, board, lantern and plants are CC0 assets from Poly Haven. Tap anywhere to close.
